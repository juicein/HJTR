Our Responsibility

Renovation is more than building a home.

For families, a renovation project is closely connected to health, safety, comfort and long-term quality of life. For the environment, renovation also creates material consumption, packaging waste and construction waste.


Sichuan Yuche Construction Engineering Co., Ltd. is developing a more responsible residential renovation approach focused on three priorities: healthy homes, transparent materials and lower-waste renovation.

Our ESG Strategy

Three pillars.
One long-term commitment.

Our sustainability framework connects environmental responsibility, social value and transparent governance with the everyday renovation experience of Chengdu families.

Healthy Home Initiative

A healthier home starts with better information.

For parenting households, renovation decisions can affect indoor air quality and everyday wellbeing. Our approach is to provide clearer information about material characteristics and encourage informed choices rather than making unsupported health claims.

Our Material Health Approach

Prioritise low-emission alternatives where suitable
Provide available product and certification information
Improve material traceability
Explain limitations and proper use
Collect customer feedback after renovation

SUPPLY CHAIN
Packaging Improvement

Less packaging.
More responsibility.

Renovation materials often require protective packaging. Our objective is not to eliminate necessary protection, but to reduce avoidable single-use materials while maintaining product safety.

01

Measure

Record major packaging materials used during selected renovation projects.

02

Reduce

Identify unnecessary layers and avoid excessive single-use protective materials.

03

Reuse

Explore reusable protection for suitable transportation and installation scenarios.

04

Recycle

Separate recyclable packaging materials at project sites where practical.
